Abstract

In sales and CRM environments, missed leads represent a significant loss of business revenue. Manual monitoring of large email inboxes is time-consuming and error-prone.

This project implements a Machine Learning Based Missed Lead Detection and Automated Follow-up System that intelligently classifies incoming emails as leads or non-leads, identifies those that have not been responded to within a defined SLA window, and automatically triggers personalized follow-up emails.

A real-world email dataset of 12,500 records was collected, pre-processed, and labelled. Multiple ML classifiers were trained and evaluated. The Random Forest classifier achieved the best performance with 96% accuracy and an F1-score of 0.95, outperforming all baselines.

The system is deployed as a full-stack web application with a React dashboard and FastAPI backend, enabling real-time monitoring and automated follow-up scheduling.

Introduction

Lead management is the backbone of modern sales pipelines. Studies show that responding to a lead within the first hour increases conversion probability by 7×. However, with hundreds of emails daily, sales teams frequently miss high-intent messages.

Traditional rule-based filters are brittle and require constant manual updates. Machine learning offers an adaptive alternative: by learning patterns from historical email data, a classifier reliably identifies potential leads regardless of phrasing or context.

Key Objectives:

  • Classify incoming emails as leads or non-leads using ML models
  • Detect emails missed (no reply within the SLA window)
  • Automatically generate and dispatch personalized follow-up emails
  • Visualize lead pipeline health via an interactive dashboard

Methods and Materials
  • Data Collection: 12,500 real-world sales and support emails assembled via Gmail API integration and public CRM datasets, annotated with binary lead/non-lead labels and reply-status flags.
  • Data Preprocessing: Lowercasing, stopword removal, URL/email stripping, and lemmatization using NLTK. Class imbalance handled via SMOTE (lead:non-lead ratio 1:4.2 before balancing).
  • Feature Engineering: TF-IDF vectors (5,000 features), email metadata (subject urgency keywords, sender domain score, time-of-day), reply latency, and sentiment polarity score using TextBlob.
  • Model Building: Trained and compared Naive Bayes, Logistic Regression, SVM, Decision Tree, and Random Forest using scikit-learn with 5-fold cross-validated GridSearchCV tuning.
  • Automated Follow-up: Missed leads (no reply within 24 h) trigger a GPT-assisted template selection pipeline; emails dispatched via SMTP with personalized, context-aware body text.
  • Evaluation: Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-score, and ROC-AUC computed on a held-out 20% test set to select the best production model.

Discussion

The Random Forest classifier achieved 96% accuracy and ROC-AUC of 0.98, significantly outperforming all baselines. Its ensemble nature handles the noisy, high-dimensional TF-IDF feature space effectively while remaining interpretable through feature importance scores.

Key factors contributing to missed lead probability were reply latency and subject urgency keywords. Emails containing terms like "quote", "pricing", "urgent", or "interested" that remained unanswered beyond 24 hours were the most at-risk, aligning with real-world sales insights.

The automated follow-up subsystem achieved a delivery rate of 98.3% and an average response-time improvement of 6.8 hours vs. manual processes in A/B testing, demonstrating measurable operational efficiency gains.

One limitation is performance degradation on highly domain-specific or multilingual corpora. Future work will explore transformer-based models (BERT/RoBERTa) for richer contextual understanding.

Conclusions

The ML-based Missed Lead Detection System successfully addresses a critical CRM gap by combining NLP-driven email classification with intelligent follow-up automation.

Random Forest with 96% accuracy and 0.98 ROC-AUC provides a robust, production-ready classifier. The end-to-end pipeline reduces manual intervention and accelerates lead response time by an average of 6.8 hours.

Future work will explore BERT-based deep learning models, multilingual support, and CRM API integration (Salesforce, HubSpot) to further enhance scalability across enterprise environments.
